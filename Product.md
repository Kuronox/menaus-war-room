# Product Vision

## Vision

War Room is a football decision support system.

Every feature must help the manager make better decisions.

Showing statistics alone is not enough.

Every screen should answer:

"What should I do now?"

---

# Weekly War Room Report

Every HRF import generates an automatic report.

The report must include:

## Club Status

- Form changes
- Skill pops
- Injuries
- Suspensions
- Financial summary
- Team spirit
- Confidence

---

## Training

The system must:

- Detect trained players
- Detect missing minutes
- Calculate remaining minutes
- Optimize league + friendly
- Prevent training waste

---

## League Lineup

Generate the strongest lineup for the official match.

Explain WHY every player is selected.

---

## Friendly Lineup

Friendly matches exist only to maximize training.

Winning is secondary.

Training is always the priority.

---

## Best XI

Generate the strongest possible lineup ignoring training.

Useful for:

- Arena matches
- Finals
- Friendly tournaments

---

## Opponent Analysis

Analyze:

- Formation
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Tactical trends
- Dangerous areas

Generate tactical recommendations.

---

## Development

Track every player's evolution.

Detect:

- Pops
- Form drops
- ROI
- Market value
- Future prospects

---

## Command Center

The home screen must always display actionable decisions.

Example:

🔴 Configure league lineup

🟡 Configure friendly lineup

🟢 Castillo close to pop

🔴 Pazour injured

🟡 Rival attacks through the wings

The manager should know exactly what needs to be done this week.

## Decision Transparency

# Decision Transparency

Uno de los principios fundamentales de Menaus War Room es que todas las recomendaciones deben ser explicables.

El sistema nunca debe limitarse a indicar una acción; también debe explicar por qué la recomienda y cuál es el nivel de confianza de dicha recomendación.

Cada recomendación deberá incluir:

## Recommendation

La acción sugerida.

## Why

Explicación del razonamiento.

## Evidence

Clasificación de las fuentes utilizadas:

★★★★★ Evidencia oficial
- Manual oficial de Hattrick
- Hattrick Wiki
- Dev Blogs
- Reglas oficiales

★★★★☆ Evidencia empírica
- HRF
- Historial del equipo
- Estadísticas propias

★★★☆☆ Consenso de la comunidad
- Foros
- Reddit
- HO!
- Foxtrick
- Experiencias ampliamente aceptadas

★★☆☆☆ Inferencia
- Deducción lógica basada en evidencia parcial.

★☆☆☆☆ Hipótesis
- No existe evidencia suficiente.
- Debe marcarse explícitamente como especulación.

Nivel de confianza:

Alta

Hipótesis
